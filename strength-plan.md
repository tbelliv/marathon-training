# Strength — Monday power / Thursday PT

Two sessions a week, 40–50 minutes each, through Ventura (Feb 21 2027). Trap bar for every barbell lift: step in, one bar, deadlift from the low handles, squat from the high handles. Core and hip work on both days.

Why this split: heavy, low-rep strength (≥80 % 1RM, 3–5 reps) and low-volume plyometrics are the two gym inputs with evidence for running economy in trained runners (Storen 2008, Spurrs 2003, Blagrove 2018, Balsalobre-Fernández 2016). The PT circuit keeps the tendons, hips and feet healthy but does not move economy; it stays, on Thursday. Monday is the day that gets heavy because it follows the Sunday long run (hard days together, Tuesday track is 24 h later and will feel it for the first three weeks, then not).

Starting point: no barbell history, 10 kg PT loads, 74 kg. Tendons adapt slower than muscles. Progress by 2.5–5 kg a week, never to failure, bar speed stays fast on every rep. If a set slows, the next set is lighter.

### Trap-bar deadlift progression (bar assumed 25 kg; weigh it)

Reference: 125 kg (275 lb) pulled on the second-ever session. Working 1RM assumed 130 kg until a heavier set says otherwise. Loads below are chosen so the January blocks sit at 85–90 % of that, which is where the economy research lives; the early weeks are light on purpose for tendons, not muscle.

| Taper | Mon Oct 12 | 3×5 | 60 kg | 45 | groove, nothing more |
| Race week | Mon Oct 19 | — | — | | no bar, no plyos. Core and hips only, 15 min |
| Recovery | Oct 26 – Nov 1 | — | — | | nothing. Walk, sleep, eat |
| 1–3 | Nov 2 – 22 | 3×8 | 70 → 80 kg | 55–60 | tendon prep, pogo hops in |

The squat tracks the deadlift at 65–70 %: goblet 16–24 kg through November, then trap-bar high handles 60–70 kg in December and 75–85 kg in January. Tonight and Oct 12 the squat is a 16 kg goblet, 3×8.

120 kg is 1.6 × bodyweight. The number is less important than the rule: if a set slows, the next set is lighter, and a week is repeated rather than skipped. A fast-twitch bias means the strength comes easily; the tendons and the Tuesday track session are the limiters, so the early weeks stay light even though they will feel easy.

## Rules

- Monday is the only heavy day. Two heavy days a week plus track and a long run is how runners get hurt in the gym.
- Nothing new inside three weeks of a race. The Oct 5 and Oct 12 bar sessions are technique at 35 kg, not training.
- Plyometric ground contacts stay under 100 per session. More is not better; the dose that improves economy is small.
- Skip Monday if the long run left anything sore beyond normal. The long run is the priority; the gym serves it.
- Log every Monday: load, reps, bar speed (fast / slowing / grinding). Grinding means the next week does not go up.
