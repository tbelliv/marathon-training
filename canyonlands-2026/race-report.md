## Read

- **Miles 1–7: executed.** Within 30 s of the card at every mile, HR 153–161, mile 2 let go at 9:23, descents clean. Through The Pit at 7.7 about a minute up on 3:20.
- **Miles 8–13: on effort, behind on pace.** HR stayed 154–161 (the bands) but the wet-rock descents at 10–12 ran 8:20 / 6:51 / 7:48 against a card of 6:53 / 6:10 / 7:14. GAP on those miles (8:54, 7:24, 8:23) says braking, not fading. 5:15 down on the card at 13, projecting ~3:27, still 20+ min under the course record and leading.
- **13.6: the cramp.** Pace 7:30 → 12:40 inside a quarter mile, HR 161 → 130 (stopped). It hit at the lowest point of the course at the foot of the long climb, after three miles of eccentric, stabilizing work on wet rock in cold, wet, compressed tissue. Inner thigh = adductor / vastus medialis, the slip-stabilizers. Classic neuromuscular cramp; not an electrolyte deficit.
- **Miles 14–17:** limping and walking, 10–14 min/mi, three stops.
- **Miles 18–20:** running again at 9–10 min/mi, HR 141–154, after the salt pills and Skratch. Then the gut went: miles 21–23 walking at 11–21 min/mi with seven stops. DNF at 22.9.

## What changes

1. **Cramp protocol (new, written into every race card):** stop, straighten the leg, stretch the muscle 20–30 s, walk 30 s, run easy 3–5 min, resume. No new fluids or pills. Pickle juice or a mustard packet if carried (reflex, tested in training first).
2. **Nothing new under duress** is the rule that broke. Salt pills and Skratch were never tested. Any race-day electrolyte must be trained with in October-style long runs first.
3. **Wet-rock descents cost ~2.5 min on the card.** In rain the card's descent paces are void; run them by HR and accept the time.
4. **Cold-wet kit for the legs:** tights or no tights are both fine, but the adductors were cold and wet for 1:45 in half tights. Test a longer tight or an embrocation on a cold-rain long run before the next cold race.
5. The fitness was real: 13 miles at race effort in those conditions, leading. Highland Park (Nov 26) and Ventura (Feb 21) stand.
