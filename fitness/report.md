## Methods and caveats

- **TRIMP** (Banister, male coefficients) from duration and average HR, HR rest 47, HR max 192. Daily load drives CTL (42-day time constant), ATL (7-day), TSB = CTL − ATL. Same model as TrainingPeaks, Intervals.icu and the Nike and D1 dashboards; the units are TRIMP, not TSS, so compare only to yourself.
- **GAP** is the Strava 2017 empirical curve as reconstructed in runningworkbench. Minetti is not used anywhere.
- **Efficiency factor and decoupling** per Friel: GAP speed over HR, first half vs second half after a 10-minute warm-up.
- **Critical speed** two-parameter linear model on best 3–30 minute efforts across all files. D′ is the anaerobic work capacity above CS, in metres.
- **VDOT** Daniels and Gilbert formulas; altitude correction ~4 % per 3,000 ft above 3,000 ft for 30–60 minute efforts. Estimates, ±5 %. A lab test replaces these.
- **Durability** EF per 30-minute block after warm-up, relative to block 1, per Jones et al. 2021 and Maunder 2021.
- Strength sessions are scored in `strength-log.md` by session RPE (Foster) and are not in the TRIMP model.
